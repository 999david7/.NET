Keepy.

A macOS reminders app. Write down what you need to remember, pick a time, and Keepy sends a notification even when the app is closed.

Get Keepy ↓ Explore features

What Keepy can do

View on GitHub ↗

Quick scheduling

Set a time directly or use presets such as 15 minutes from now.

System notifications

Reminders appear through macOS notifications at the scheduled time.

Local reminders

Keepy stores reminders on your Mac and shows sent reminders in a separate list.
